name: Service grouping rules
description: The maintainer's product rule for the Social Downloaders category.
---

"Social downloader should be only social media app." Play Store is not a social-media app.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the category because unrelated services had been grouped under Social Downloaders.

**How to apply:** Group by the service's actual purpose, not whether its endpoint can download something. Keep app stores and unrelated tools outside Social Downloaders when adding or regrouping routes.
